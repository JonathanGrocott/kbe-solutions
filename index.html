Fit Check

Is this a fit for your plant?

This works best for teams that already have data but struggle to make consistent decisions quickly. If approvals and exceptions depend on a few experts, we can help you standardize and speed up.

Typical starting point

One line, one process, one KPI target: decision cycle time, rework rate, or escalation volume.

Good fit if...

  • You rely on email, meetings, and tribal knowledge for exception decisions
  • Approval delays impact throughput, delivery, or quality costs
  • You have 1-2 operations leaders available weekly for pilot feedback
  • You want measurable results before scaling to more lines or plants

Not ideal yet if...

  • You are still defining the core process and do not have stable ownership
  • No one can dedicate at least 2 hours per week to pilot decisions
  • Your team is not ready to adopt any workflow changes during the pilot window
  • You need a full ERP or MES replacement instead of a decision layer

FAQ

Questions we hear from smaller manufacturers

We do not have MES. Can we still start?

Yes. Many pilots begin with ERP, QMS, spreadsheets, and operator notes.

How much IT effort is required?

Typically light support for read access and validation, not a full integration project.

How quickly will we see value?

Most teams see measurable decision-cycle improvements during a 4-6 week pilot.

Do we need to change all workflows?

No. We start with one high-friction workflow and scale after results are proven.

What happens after the pilot?

You get KPI outcomes, rollout recommendations, and a clear expansion or stop decision.

Can AI make decisions without oversight?

Not by default. Human-in-the-loop approvals and guardrails are part of the operating model.
