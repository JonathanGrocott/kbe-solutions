KBE Solutions - Sample Pilot Brief
Version: 1.0

Objective
- Improve one high-friction manufacturing workflow in 4-6 weeks.
- Deliver measurable KPI movement before any scale decision.

Recommended First Workflow
- Engineering change control, CAPA triage, maintenance escalation, or supplier exception handling.

Pilot Scope
- One plant, one process owner, one KPI target.
- Existing systems only (ERP/QMS/MES/spreadsheets/email notes).
- No rip-and-replace implementation.

Timeline
- Week 1: Discovery, baseline KPI definition, and pilot success criteria.
- Week 2-3: Decision map + context model setup.
- Week 4-5: Guided usage with real workflow events.
- Week 6: KPI readout and go/no-go recommendation.

Inputs Required From Your Team
- Process owner (1-2 hours/week).
- Quality or operations lead (1 hour/week).
- IT/data contact for read access validation (light touch).

Deliverables
- Searchable decision history with rationale and precedent links.
- Pilot KPI baseline vs. observed results.
- Rollout recommendation: expand, refine, or stop.
- Exportable handoff documentation.

Pilot Success Criteria (example)
- 20%+ improvement in decision cycle time.
- Reduced repeat escalations for same issue class.
- Clear ownership and approval paths documented.

Commercial Structure (directional)
- Pilot engagement: low five figures, fixed scope.
- Expansion: subscription scales by workflows/locations covered.

Risk Controls
- NDA available before technical sharing.
- Scope and acceptance criteria documented in SOW.
- Expand only if target KPI movement is demonstrated.

Next Step
- Share your workflow and KPI target; we return a scoped pilot plan.
